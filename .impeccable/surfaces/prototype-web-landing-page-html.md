Audience: parents of collège students (14 to 16) in Agadir, and the students themselves.
Action: book an appointment / enrol for 2026-2027, or ask for app access. No app-store link exists, so none is invented.
Proof on hand:
- real website copy and parent testimonials
- the methods, programmes and the multidisciplinary team
- the student app (screens exported in `resources/screens/v3-college/`)

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the carnet de liaison between EducazenKids and the family: dated entries from the centre, each proving one feature, closed by the parents' signature that books the appointment. It refuses the hero-plus-feature-cards school site.

OWN-WORLD:
- The student app's world: lavender ground, plum-ink, brand purple, Kids pink, teal, gold; Fredoka display and Quicksand text.
- Printed onto a school notebook: white pages with Seyès ruling in lavender, a pink margin line, Caveat handwriting for the centre's notes and signatures, date stamps, a steel paperclip holding the phone.
- Committed colour: the first viewport's desk is brand purple.

STORY: Families see that the centre teaches each child at their own pace, that the app counts real effort (quiz unlocked at 50 % watched, résumés, timetable, notifications), and that a team follows each child. They sign to book an appointment.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Top bar: logo, links, « Espace équipe » icon button, « Prendre rendez-vous ».
- On a purple desk, an open spread about 1100 px wide. Left page: « Mot aux parents », a Fredoka headline around 56 px, two lines of body, the primary action. Right page: the real app home screen on a phone clipped to the page, and a « Rentrée 2026-2027 » date stamp.

FORM: Liaison notebook, position 2 of 7 on the ranked surface list, seed key 6a847ee3. Signature interaction: the signature line writes the typed name in handwriting and becomes the appointment request.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
