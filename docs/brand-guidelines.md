## Story, mission, vision

- **Story:** the founder is the mother of an atypical child, who faced schools that refused him: « EducaZenKids, c'est le lieu que j'aurais aimé trouver pour mon enfant et pour moi. »
- **Mission:** « Permettre à chaque enfant de reprendre goût à l'apprentissage, dans un environnement bienveillant, stimulant et inclusif. »
- **Vision:** « Devenir une référence de l'éducation inclusive au Maroc. »
- **Promise:** « Chaque enfant mérite un espace où il peut évoluer à son rythme, avec un accompagnement pensé pour sa singularité. »
- **Three poles:** Apprentissage scolaire (Montessori, Freinet and Steiner, adapted), Formation parents, Pôle psychosocial (a multidisciplinary and paramedical team).

## Audiences

- **The app today targets collège students, 14 to 16 years** (3e, CNED programme). The owner decided this on 11/10/2026. They want it « a little mature » compared with the first kids demo. Profiles: typique, DYS, TDAH, TSA, HPI.
- **Earlier exploration (archived):** a kids app for 4 to 8 years (`resources/screens/v1-english/`). It used audio and pictures for non-readers, an owl mascot and chunky 3D buttons.
- **Parents:** they follow progress. The center offers « Coaching parental »; a parent space is not built yet.

## Tone of voice

- **Language:** French first, since the school, Instagram and website are all French. The center also has an Arabic phone line, so Arabic (RTL) is a likely second language.
- **Student-facing text (collège):** use « tu ». Be direct and respectful, never childish. Use short sentences, real subject vocabulary (coefficient, contrôle, TP), and no baby talk. Examples: « Ton parcours sur mesure », « Quiz à 50 % · encore 4:40 », « Progression enregistrée ».
- **Parent-facing text:** use « vous », the way the website addresses parents. Keep it reassuring and concrete.
- **Praise effort, not only results:** « Bravo, tu maîtrises les points clés ». After a mistake: « Pas tout à fait », with the explanation (never « Faux »).
- **Emoji:** none in the collège UI; use line icons. Emoji (🌱 is the brand emoji) belong to the kids version and social media.

### App usage (target v2 palette, in `resources/design-tokens.json`)

- **White and purple carry the UI (about 85%):** white `#FFFFFF` pages, soft purple `#FAF5FF` / `#F3E8FF` surfaces, brand purple `#9333EA` (close to the logo's « Educazen » purple) for buttons and active states, and deep purple `#3B0764` for text.
- **Pink `#F24CC3`, the logo's « Kids »:** child-facing highlights and celebrations. Use it sparingly, never on large surfaces.
- **Gold `#F2AB19`:** stars and rewards only.
- **Teal `#088D84`:** correct answers and success, replacing the earlier green.
- **Plum `#BC4384`, the logo's head:** optional accent in the parent space.

A DYS-friendly reading font (OpenDyslexic or Lexend) is planned as an option in the parent space. It is not part of the brand fonts.

## Logo usage

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `resources/brand/logo-educative.jpeg` | Original, 1500×1500, white background. |
| `resources/brand/logo-educazenkids.png` | Cropped full logo, transparent background, 1123×695. For welcome screens and documents. |
| `resources/brand/logo-mark.png` | The child's head with the puzzle heart, transparent, 436×468. For the app icon, avatars and small spaces. |

- Place the logo on white or very light backgrounds only. The transparent PNGs were cut from a JPEG, so dark backgrounds show a faint halo.
- Minimum widths: 120 px for the full logo, 32 px for the mark. Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the « E ».
- Never recolor, stretch, rotate or add effects.
- **No vector version exists yet.** Ask the owner for an SVG or AI file before any large print or app-store asset.

## Mascot

**Kids version only.** The collège app has no mascot. The kids version has an owl in a graduation cap (`resources/illustrations/mascot-owl.svg`, in brand colors). It has no official name: the v1 demo called it « Ollie », but that is not validated, so don't use the name in French copy without the owner's approval. It reassures, gives hints and celebrates, and it never scolds.
